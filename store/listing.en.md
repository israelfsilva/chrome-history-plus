## Description

```
History Plus puts your Chrome browsing history in the side panel, so you can find what you visited without leaving the tab you're on.

FILTER BY DAY OR DATE RANGE
Chrome's history page has no date picker. History Plus adds a calendar: pick a single day, choose a start and end date, or step back and forth one day at a time with the arrows.

EXACT VISIT TIMES
Chrome's history search only reports the most recent visit of each page. History Plus looks up every visit inside the dates you select, so a page you opened on three different days shows up on all three, each with its own time.

FAST SEARCH
Search by page title or address. Accents and capitalization don't matter, and search works together with the date filter.

QUICK ACTIONS
• Click a page to open it in a new tab (Ctrl/Cmd+click opens it in the background).
• Remove a page from your history and undo within 5 seconds.

FITS RIGHT IN
• Light and dark themes that follow Chrome.
• English and Brazilian Portuguese.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything runs on your device. History Plus makes no network requests, has no analytics and never sends your history anywhere. It's open source: https://github.com/israelfsilva/chrome-history-plus
```
