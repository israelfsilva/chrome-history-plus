# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-21

First public release.

- Browsing history in Chrome's side panel, grouped by day.
- Filter by a single day or a date range, with day-by-day navigation.
- Exact visit times inside the selected dates (resolves every visit, not only the latest).
- Search by title or address, ignoring accents and capitalization, combined with the date filter.
- Remove a page from history with a 5-second undo.
- Light and dark themes; English and Brazilian Portuguese.
